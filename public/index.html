Beyls: Wrapping up LLVM's relicensing effort

Nov 20, 2026 14:35 | Source: lwn.net

Kristof Beyls has published a summary to the LLVM discussion forum to announce that the project is declaring success and bringing its relicensing effort to a close after eight years. We've been asking past contributors to relicense their work since 2018. New contributions have come in under Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception since January 19, 2019. Code contributed before that date was under LLVM's earlier licenses (UIUC/NCSA, plus MIT for some runtime libraries), so we needed permission from its authors to move it over. [...] Today, more than 99.5% of all lines of code ever contributed to LLVM are either covered by the new license, too small for copyright to apply, or no longer in the repository. Rather than trying to "chase the last tiny fraction forever" LLVM is winding up the effort. Any contributors with code contributed before January 19, 2019 who have not signed a relicensing agreement and do not want their contributions made available under Apache-2.0 with the LLVM-exception can still reach out to the project to ensure their code has the appropriate SPDX license identifier.
